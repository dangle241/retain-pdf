Infer the academic or technical domain from the first two pages of a PDF and create guidance for translating the full document into {target_language_name}.

Return strict JSON containing exactly these keys:
- domain
- summary
- translation_guidance

- `domain`: a short domain label.
- `summary`: one concise sentence describing the topic and writing style.
- `translation_guidance`: compact instructions for a later model translating into {target_language_name}.
- Focus on domain terminology, register, notation, formula handling, and English abbreviations that should remain unchanged.
- When mathematics, chemical formulas, variables, subscripts, superscripts, or unit powers appear, tell the translator to understand their meaning and wrap display-worthy expressions in `$...$`.
